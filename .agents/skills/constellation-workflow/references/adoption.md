# Adoption and naming boundaries

Inspect the target project's instructions and adoption record; do not create or change adoption just to make this workflow run. Axis, Meridian and Sentinel can be used independently without adopting Polaris. When governance is absent, follow the project's process; when unclear and consequential, resolve the ambiguity before claiming compliance.

For an explicit Polaris adopter, read its adopted Constitution and operating rules and the composition contract at the revision used. Canon is currently `polaris/constitution/` in pedrobritx/polaris; verify the adopted revision rather than assuming a path. Trace consequential choices to applicable principles. Preserve complete adopted policies, human gates and required continuity. If the adopted composition contract requires planner output, use its documented version-1 planner with the real request and selected enabled disciplines. Do not impose experimental A0 intake/results/receipts unless adopted or requested. If a hard requirement conflicts with this lightweight flow, cite it and propose a bounded operational exception for human approval; do not silently waive or amend it.

The existing Polaris `.agents/skills/constellation-a0` entry point serves explicit A0 routing and evidence-contract work. Use it when that contract is requested/adopted, rather than loading both workflows by default. `constellation-workflow` supplies the conversational product loop; it does not replace or certify A0.

Pedro distinguishes BSDL (philosophical/design foundation), Meridian (holistic product/design methodology) and BSF (software-building processes). Current repository contracts name Polaris as constitutional/governance owner and Meridian as interface-method capability. Preserve those boundaries and acknowledge this naming mismatch when it affects a decision. This workflow neither equates the concepts nor rebrands or ratifies their canon.

Capability discovery starts at the existing project references or the owning repositories: [Polaris](https://github.com/pedrobritx/polaris), [Axis](https://github.com/pedrobritx/axis), [Meridian](https://github.com/pedrobritx/meridian), [Sentinel](https://github.com/pedrobritx/sentinel). Follow selected entry points and their required reading; record immutable revisions for evidence. Main-branch links are navigation aids, not proof of reviewed content.
