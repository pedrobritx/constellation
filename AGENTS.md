# Constellation contributor entry point

Read README.md, docs/product.md, constellation.json and the actual affected source before working. Polaris is the default coordinator for this product. Read its adopted Constitution/operating rules and composition contract at the revision used for the task; preserve the host instruction and permission hierarchy.

Use only enabled disciplines required by the task, following their own AGENTS.md and discipline.json. Keep request IDs, product context, acceptance criteria, source revisions and evidence linked. Excluded scopes remain visible until alternative coverage is verified. Direct discipline use in other projects does not adopt Constellation or Polaris implicitly.

Keep product-specific application code, designs, decisions, threat models and evidence here. Improve reusable Axis/Meridian/Sentinel capabilities in their owning repositories. Preserve each product's technology and identity; do not copy entire template catalogues or promote experimental design by default.

There is currently no application or build/test toolchain in this repository. Do not claim a builder, AI delegation service, authentication system, deployment or assurance exists because documentation describes it. Add only the implementation and checks justified by an approved concrete product task. Do not provision, deploy, publish, merge or change security posture without applicable authorisation. Write code, comments, technical instructions and reviews in English.
